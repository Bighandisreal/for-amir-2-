01

01 / the beginning

This is not a birthday letter.

I could have written you the usual thing. Happy birthday. I wish you happiness, success, good health, and all the other things people put into birthday messages because they are expected to say them.

But you know me well enough to know that I would find that terribly boring.

So, keep this instead.

02

02 / the person I met

The Person I Met

There are people you meet and eventually forget small things about.

Then there are people whose existence somehow leaves little marks everywhere.

You became one of those people.

Not because you tried particularly hard to be memorable. You probably didn't.

You simply existed as yourself, and somehow that was enough.

03

03 / things you probably don't notice

Things You Probably Don't Notice

✦ You have a habit of taking care of everyone while forgetting that you are also someone who needs taking care of.
♡ There were times you were so busy with school that you forgot to eat.
🌷 There were times you slept at school because apparently sleeping properly was optional.
☾ And yes, I remember that night you were still awake around 2 AM.

I noticed. I just didn't make a spectacle of it.

04

04 / things I haven't forgotten

Things I Have Not Forgotten

I remember the little things more than I remember the grand ones.

  • the random conversations
  • the stupid jokes
  • the moments you were tired but still showed up
  • the times you probably didn't realize I was worried
  • the way you somehow make ordinary conversations memorable

Memory is a terrible archivist. It loses dates, details, and entire conversations.

But some people stay.

“Some forms of love are inconveniently practical.”

05

05 / for the version of you...

For the Version of You Who Thinks She Has Fallen Behind

You are allowed to have days where you feel like everyone else is moving faster than you.

You are allowed to be tired. You are allowed to not have everything figured out.

Just don't turn one difficult season into a permanent description of yourself.

You are still becoming.

06

06 / for the happy version

For the Version of You Who Is Happy

This one is simpler.

When something good happens to you, don't immediately search for the part that could go wrong.

Let yourself have the good thing.

You don't need to earn every happy moment by suffering first.

Keep the good days, too.

07

07 / something I want you to know

Something I Want You to Know

I don't keep people around simply because we've known each other for a long time.

I keep people around because I find them worth the inconvenience of caring.

You matter to me.

That's really all there is to it.

“You matter to me.”

08

08 / if we ever become strangers

If We Ever Become Strangers

If life gets busy enough that we eventually become strangers again, I hope you know that it doesn't erase what existed before it.

There will still have been a version of my life where you were here.

A version where I knew you. Where I worried about whether you had eaten. Where I knew you stayed awake too late.

And I think that's worth remembering.

09

09 / a note to future Raiah

A Note to Future Raiah

Hi.

I don't know when you're reading this.

Maybe you're still studying. Maybe you've graduated. Maybe your life looks completely different now.

Whatever happened between now and then, I hope you didn't become too hard on yourself.

And if you did, I hope you eventually learned how to be gentle with yourself again.

Take care of yourself, Lola.

10

10 / if I'm far away

If I'm Far Away

If you read this while I'm somewhere far away, don't make the distance heavier than it needs to be.

Distance is not disappearance.

People can be far from each other and still remain important.

So don't read this like a goodbye.

I'd rather say “see you later” and mean it.

11 / lastly

Lastly

♡

Happy birthday, Raiah.

Keep this little corner of the internet.

Come back to it whenever you need to remember that someone once noticed the little things.

I'll see you later, Lola.
